Our Documentation Methodology

AHRW follows a systematic process to collect, verify, and analyze information concerning human rights violations.

01

Testimonies

We first collect testimonies and firsthand accounts from people who have experienced or witnessed human rights violations.

02

Media Monitoring

We collect relevant information and reports published by news media and other reliable sources.

03

Official Statements

We review and analyze statements and information released by governments, authorities, and relevant organizations.

04

Cross-Check

We cross-check testimonies, media reports, and official statements to assess consistency and credibility.

05

Analysis

We analyze verified information to identify incidents, patterns, trends, and emerging human rights concerns.

Our Work

Our work focuses on collecting testimonies, verifying information, analyzing incidents, preserving documentation, and supporting human rights research and accountability efforts.

Testimony Collection

Collecting testimonies and firsthand accounts from people affected by human rights violations.

Verification

Cross-checking information and available evidence to strengthen the credibility of documented incidents.

Analysis

Analyzing incidents and human rights developments to identify patterns, trends, and emerging concerns.

Mission

Our Mission

To document, verify, and preserve credible information on human rights violations, while supporting efforts toward truth, justice, and accountability.

Vision

Our Vision

A society where human rights are respected, violations are documented, victims are heard, and perpetrators are held accountable.
